About Me

Olanrewaju Sulyman Lanre is a PMP-certified Senior IT Infrastructure Project Manager and technology leader with extensive experience across enterprise IT infrastructure, telecommunications, cybersecurity, satellite communications and digital transformation.

He has successfully delivered more than 500 enterprise technology projects and managed an estimated USD $200 million project portfolio while working with government agencies, telecommunications companies, energy organizations, healthcare institutions, higher education and multinational organizations.

His expertise combines executive leadership, technical knowledge, project governance, commercial management and stakeholder engagement to deliver secure, resilient and scalable technology solutions.
